Privacy

SequelBridge collects nothing about you or your databases.

Last updated October 2026

No accountNothing to sign up for or sign in to.
No analytics or trackingThe app doesn't measure or follow what you do.
No adsNone in the app, and nothing shared with advertisers.
No crash reportingThe app sends no reports of its own when something goes wrong.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your connections, statement history and saved queries are files on your Mac.
  • Passwords are kept in your Mac's Keychain, never in those files.
  • The App Store version keeps the same files in its own folder, inside the sandbox macOS gives App Store apps.

What the app connects to

  • The database and SSH servers you add, when you connect to them, and nothing else.
  • The version from this site or Homebrew checks for a new version once a day, if you allow it, by reading the update feed on the project's GitHub releases. The App Store version is updated by the App Store.
  • Links in the Help menu open the guide, GitHub and this site in your browser.

You can check it

SequelBridge is free and open source under the MIT license. Its source code is on GitHub, so anyone can confirm what it does with your data.
