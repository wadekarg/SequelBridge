SequelBridge

Privacy

October 2026

SequelBridge collects no data. It has no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting, and it sends nothing about you or your databases to anyone.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your connections, statement history and saved queries are files on your Mac.
  • Passwords are kept in your Mac's Keychain.

What the app connects to

  • The database and SSH servers you configure, and nothing else.
  • The version from this site or Homebrew also checks for a new version once a day, when you allow it, by reading the update feed on the project's GitHub releases. The App Store version is updated by the App Store.
